A few weeks of adventure

Goals range from 30–40 miles. Four 2-mile walks each week make an adventure last about 4–5 weeks. At 1 mile per walk, allow about twice as long; at 3 miles, about two-thirds as long.

These are invented walking distances, loosely scaled to the selected series. They aren’t distances from the books.

How to use Storystride
  1. Choose one of the four adventures, or create your own.
  2. Walk at your own pace, then enter miles or kilometers, a date, and an optional note.
  3. Choose which adventure receives those miles and save your walk.
  4. Follow your marker along the route. Reach a checkpoint to reveal its story and earn a badge.
  5. Export a backup to keep a portable copy of your progress.
Costs & limitations

This self-contained version needs no subscription, paid API, or server. All artwork and checkpoint writing are original.

There is no GPS, automatic step counting, fitness integration, account, or device syncing. Manual entry is the working version; integrations are future possibilities.

Clearing browser data, using private browsing, or moving this file can lose access to saved progress. Local-file storage varies by browser. Export first and restore in the new location.

A hosted version may depend on its provider’s free limits. Custom domains, store distribution, and future cloud services can have costs; none are needed to use this local version.
